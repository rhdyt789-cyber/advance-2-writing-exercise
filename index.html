Online communication has become increasingly important in modern society. Many people use messaging apps, social media, and video calls daily.

One advantage is that people can communicate across different countries without travelling. Furthermore, families and friends can maintain close relationships despite living far apart.

On the other hand, some experts believe that excessive online communication may reduce important face-to-face social skills.

On balance, online communication offers significant benefits, but people should not ignore the value of direct personal interaction.

Part A: Reading Comprehension

1. What are two examples of online communication?
2. What is one advantage mentioned?
3. What concern do experts have?
4. What is the author's final opinion?
